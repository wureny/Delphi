**Updated:** July 13, 2026  
**Purpose:** distinguish current official facts, internal decisions, planning assumptions, prototype evidence, and missing founder inputs.

---

## 1. Evidence levels

### A — Verified external

Current official or authoritative source checked for the specific claim.

### B — Internal primary

Current Delphi founding document, architecture, repository evidence, or founder-provided fact.

### C — Planning assumption

Useful for execution, but not yet commercially validated.

### D — Historical prototype evidence

A capability or result from an earlier prototype. It cannot automatically be described as current company-product capability.

### E — Founder input required

No reliable source has been provided. Do not infer.

## 8. Citation and claims rule

External materials should:

- cite official competitor pages for what competitors claim;
- use independent sources for scale or funding where relevant;
- state market figures as internal bottom-up assumptions;
- distinguish target architecture from implemented capability;
- distinguish historical prototype from current product;
- date all time-sensitive facts;
- remove or recheck current claims before submission if sources change.
